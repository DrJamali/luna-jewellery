Privacy policy

Your trust matters to us. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it when you shop with Luna Jewellery.

Who we are

Luna Jewellery (“Luna”, “we”, “us”) is a celestial fine-jewellery house based at DHA Phase 3, Lahore, Pakistan. You can reach us any time at hello@luna.pk or on WhatsApp at +92 300 1234567.

Information we collect

We only collect what we need to serve you well:

  • Contact details you share with us — name, email, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Order details such as the pieces you enquire about or purchase, and the amount paid (in PKR).
  • Messages you send us by form, email, or WhatsApp.
  • Basic usage data such as the pages you view, to help us improve the site.

How we use it

  • To process and deliver your order and arrange in-store collection at DHA Phase 3.
  • To reply to your enquiries and offer styling or sizing help.
  • To send you new drops and offers — only if you ask us to. You can opt out at any time.
  • To keep our store and website safe and working properly.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your data. We share it only with trusted partners who help us run the business — for example courier services to deliver your order, or payment providers to take a payment. They may only use it to perform that task.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies to keep the site running and to understand which pieces people love most. You can clear or block cookies in your browser at any time; the site will still work.

Keeping your data safe

We protect your information with reasonable security measures and keep it only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law in Pakistan.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Just email hello@luna.pk and we will help.
