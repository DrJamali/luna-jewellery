Refund & returns

We want you to love your Luna piece. If something is not right, here is how we will make it right. All prices and refunds are in PKR (Rs).

Returns & exchanges

You may return or exchange an eligible piece within 7 days of receiving it, provided it is unworn, undamaged, and in its original Luna packaging with the care card.

What can be returned

  • Eligible: ready-to-wear pieces from the collection in original condition.
  • Not eligible: custom or made-to-order pieces, engraved or personalised items, and earrings (for hygiene reasons), unless they arrived faulty.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will confirm by email or WhatsApp. Approved refunds are issued in PKR to your original payment method within 7–10 working days. Bank or payment-gateway timings may add a few days. Original delivery charges are non-refundable unless the item was faulty or incorrect.

Exchanges & store credit

Prefer a different piece or size? We are happy to exchange, or offer store credit you can use in person at DHA Phase 3 or online. Any price difference is settled in PKR.

Faulty or incorrect items

If your piece arrives faulty or we sent the wrong item, contact us within 48 hours with a photo and we will arrange a free replacement, repair, or full refund including delivery.

Our lifetime promise

Every Luna piece comes with complimentary re-polishing and repairs for life. This is separate from returns — bring your piece to the atelier any time to bring back its shine.

How to start a return

  • Email hello@luna.pk or WhatsApp +92 300 1234567 with your order details.
  • We will share the return address or arrange in-store drop-off at DHA Phase 3, Lahore.
  • Pack the piece safely in its original box. Return shipping is the customer’s responsibility unless the item was faulty.
